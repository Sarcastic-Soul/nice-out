# Design direction: Nice Out

- **Who and where:** one person on a phone, standing at the door or already outside in bright sun, looking for ~10 seconds. Desktop is secondary.
- **Feel reference:** a printed almanac / tide table: one clear answer, then a dense, honest table of hours.
- **Type:** one display font with character + one quiet body font; big size contrast. One accent color, tinted neutrals.
- **Must NOT look like:** a weather app with a sky gradient, glassy cards, or a dashboard of widgets. The screen should feel finished in one glance, because the point is to put the phone away.
